About me

I am a PhD researcher at the Centre Inria de l'Université de Lille, in the ZEF research team, co-directed with Antonine University in Lebanon. My work is supervised by Nathalie Mitton and Carol Habib in France, and by Jad Nassar and Joseph Asmar in Lebanon.

My research focuses on wireless sensor networks for monitoring photovoltaic systems. I work on decentralized, energy-efficient node selection and data transmission mechanisms that reduce redundant communication while keeping monitoring reliable.

Alongside my research, I teach C++ programming labs to first year Computer and Communication Engineering students at Antonine University. Before starting my PhD, I worked as a full-stack developer, and I obtained my B.E. in Computer and Communication Engineering (Software Engineering and Networks) from Antonine University in 2024.
